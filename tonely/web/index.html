tonely hear how it feels

Drop in audio.
Watch its emotions move.

tonely listens with five independent analyzers: the voice, the words, the music, the acoustics and the silence. It fuses them into one live emotional timeline you can play back.

  • Voicewav2vec2 speech-emotion models read tone of voice, both categorically and as valence/arousal
  • WordsWhisper transcribes (and translates), then RoBERTa reads emotion and sentiment in what's said
  • SoundCLAP compares the audio against language descriptions of mood, for music and non-speech audio
  • Acousticsloudness, brightness, pitch, tempo, key and mode give a model-free prosody read
  • Fusiona Kalman smoother and a sticky HMM combine them, weighted by how confident each source is
